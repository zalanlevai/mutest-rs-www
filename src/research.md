# Research

**mutest-rs** is used in [active research](https://github.com/rust-mutation-testing/publications) by the Software Testing Group in the School of Computer Science at The University of Sheffield.

**mutest-rs** has been used in the following research works:

* **A Comprehensive Empirical and Theoretical Analysis of Batching Algorithms for Efficient, Safe, Parallel Mutation Analysis in Rust**<br>
  *Zalán Lévai, Donghwan Shin, Phil McMinn*<br>
  ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology (TOSEM), Just Accepted, 2026<br>
  [[DOI 10.1145/3787851](https://doi.org/10.1145/3787851)]

* **Batching Non-Conflicting Mutations for Efficient, Safe, Parallel Mutation Analysis in Rust**<br>
  *Zalán Lévai, Phil McMinn*<br>
  International Conference on Software Testing (ICST), 2023<br>
  [[DOI 10.1109/ICST57152.2023.00014](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICST57152.2023.00014)]
